# Functional Requirements (FR List)

- **FR-01 (Vehicle Search):** A customer shall be able to search and list available, non-overlapping vehicles by rental start/end dates and vehicle type (e.g., sedan, SUV, electric)[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span).
- **FR-02 (Create Reservation):** A customer shall be able to create a reservation for an available vehicle and date range; the system must prevent double-booking the same vehicle for overlapping dates[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span)[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span).
- **FR-03 (Additional Services):** A customer shall be able to select optional add-ons during reservation, such as insurance, GPS, or child seat[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span)[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span).
- **FR-04 (Price Calculation):** The system shall calculate the total rental price based on rental duration (days), vehicle type, active pricing/discount strategy, and selected additional services[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span)[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span)[span_38](start_span)[span_38](end_span).
- **FR-05 (Payment and Invoice):** A customer shall be able to complete a (simulated) payment for a reservation, record the payment/invoice, and update the reservation state[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span)[span_40](start_span)[span_40](end_span)[span_41](start_span)[span_41](end_span).
- **FR-06 (Cancel Reservation):** A customer or authorized user shall be able to cancel an eligible reservation and release/update vehicle availability[span_42](start_span)[span_42](end_span)[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span).
- **FR-07 (Vehicle Pickup and Return):** Vehicles shall be picked up and returned, updating both vehicle status (available, rented, maintenance) and reservation lifecycle state[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span)[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span)[span_46](start_span)[span_46](end_span).
- **FR-08 (Late Return Fee):** If a vehicle is returned past its scheduled return time, the system shall calculate and apply a late return fee based on the delay[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span)[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span).
- **FR-09 (Notifications and Auditing):** Reservation lifecycle events (such as creation, confirmation, cancellation, or return) shall trigger corresponding notification and audit logging observers without tight coupling[span_49](start_span)[span_49](end_span)[span_50](start_span)[span_50](end_span)[span_51](start_span)[span_51](end_span).
- **FR-10 (Customer Management):** The system shall store and manage customer registration, driver's license details, and contact information[span_52](start_span)[span_52](end_span)[span_53](start_span)[span_53](end_span).
- **FR-11 (Admin Fleet and Pricing Management):** An administrator shall be able to add/remove vehicles, update vehicle status, and manage pricing rules or discounts[span_54](start_span)[span_54](end_span)[span_55](start_span)[span_55](end_span)[span_56](start_span)[span_56](end_span).
- **FR-12 (Admin Reservations and Reports):** An administrator shall be able to view all reservations and summary reports[span_57](start_span)[span_57](end_span)[span_58](start_span)[span_58](end_span).
